AI can decide.
QED makes it prove.

An AI agent runs a treasury, but it never holds the authority. The owner signs what it may do; an independent co-signer re-derives every decision; the contracts enforce the limits and check, in the same transaction, that each conversion got within one part per million of the best price any route could give.

    Certified execution

    The route is the agent's choice. Its quality is not.

    The check QEDExecution runs in every trade

    For any prices ν, no route through the owner's constant-product venues can return more than

    B(ν) = νin·A + Σvenues [(√(νbRb) − √(νaRa/γ))₊² + (√(νaRa) − √(νbRb/γ))₊²]

    The contract recomputes B from live reserves, rounding every step upward, and accepts only if out·10⁶ ≥ B·(10⁶ − τ), τ = 1 ppm. Any ν is a valid bound, so a proof cannot be forged; at the right ν the bound meets the optimum. Scope: the five owner-approved venues, not all of DeFi.
